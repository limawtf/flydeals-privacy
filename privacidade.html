Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who processes your data

This policy applies to the FlyDeals app and website, operated by Voo4U Turismo LTDA ("FlyDeals"), in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018). Privacy questions and data subject requests can be sent to our data protection officer (DPO) at contato@fly.deals.

2. What data we collect

We collect only what each feature requires:

Account: name and email, and the login identifier when you sign in with Apple or Google.

FlyPoints: CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID) and date of birth, only if you join the program.

Balances you enter: the miles balances you record in the app, used to calculate and show offers.

Loyalty program account: if you request a points transfer, the access and identification details of your loyalty program account needed to carry it out.

Pix payments: payments are processed by a payment partner. We receive the confirmation and transaction data, and we do not store banking credentials.

Subscription: purchases and subscriptions made through the App Store are processed by Apple and managed by RevenueCat.

Notifications: the push token, if you allow notifications.

Approximate location: collected on demand, only when you use a feature that asks for it and you allow it in the system prompt.

Preferences: routes and airports of interest.

Usage and diagnostics: usage and crash events from the app and website, via PostHog, Firebase/Google Analytics and Sentry.

Advertising: ad events are sent to Meta only if you grant iOS tracking permission (ATT).

3. Why we use it and legal basis

Name, email and login: to create and maintain your account, authenticate you and provide support (performance of a contract).

CPF and date of birth: to identify each FlyPoints participant, apply the program rules and prevent fraud (consent given when you join). In the web pre-registration, this data is kept for up to 30 days to record your request; pre-registration does not activate membership or credit points.

Balances, loyalty program account and Pix: to show offers and carry out the transfers and payments you request (performance of a contract).

Push token: to send offer and route alerts (consent, given in the system permission).

Approximate location: to provide the feature that requests it, at the time of use (consent).

Usage and diagnostics: to fix errors, understand usage and improve the product (legitimate interest).

Ad events to Meta: to measure campaigns (consent, via the tracking permission). Without the permission, we do not send them.

4. Where data is stored and who receives it

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and Supabase, which act as processors for FlyDeals. We do not sell personal data. We share it only with processors, each for its own purpose:

Google (Firebase and Google Analytics): database, authentication and analytics. PostHog (US): product analytics, with sensitive data masked. Sentry: crash monitoring. Apple and RevenueCat: sign-in, subscription and purchase. Pix payment partner: payment processing. Meta: ad events, only with tracking permission. Supabase: backend and notification delivery. Cloudflare: website hosting and security.

5. Your rights

You may request confirmation and access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent (for example FlyPoints, notifications and tracking) and portability, subject to any legal retention duty. You can withdraw tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking.

Requests can be made in the app, under My account, or by email to contato@fly.deals.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep data for as long as needed for the purposes of this policy or the period required by law, whichever is longer.

You can delete your account inside the app, under My account. When you do, associated data is removed or anonymized, except what must be kept by legal obligation, such as a payment tax record.

7. Security

We adopt reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or leakage, including access control and the use of providers with their own security policies.

8. Children

FlyDeals is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. The current version is always the one published on this page, with its date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions, data subject requests or privacy complaints: contato@fly.deals.
